# DepEd Lesson Plan Standards & Architectural Guide

This skill governs the structure, pedagogy, multilingual rendering, and export requirements for Daily Lesson Logs (DLL) and Detailed Lesson Plans (DLP) in GuroDesk.

---

## 1. Official DepEd Lesson Plan Structure (DO 42, s. 2016)

Every standard DepEd Lesson Plan is structured into standard procedural blocks:

### I. Objectives (Layunin)
- **Content Standards (Pamantayang Pangnilalaman)**: Essential knowledge and concepts learners demonstrate.
- **Performance Standards (Pamantayan sa Pagganap)**: Skills and capabilities learners can perform with the content.
- **Learning Competencies (Mga Kasanayan sa Pagkatuto)**: Specific verified DepEd competency codes and descriptions.
- **Specific Learning Objectives (Mga Layunin)**: Cognitive, psychomotor, and affective operational objectives for the session.

### II. Content / Subject Matter (Nilalaman)
- Topic name, specific subtopic, subject integration (cross-curricular links), and value focus.

### III. Learning Resources (Kagamitang Panturo)
- Teacher's Guide (TG) pages, Learner's Materials (LM) pages, Textbook references, Additional materials from Learning Resource (LR) portal, and Multimedia/Concrete materials.

### IV. Procedures (Pamamaraan)
1. **Before the Lesson**:
   - Review of previous lesson / Drill / Energizer
   - Establishing purpose / Motivation (Hook)
2. **During the Lesson**:
   - Presenting examples / instances of the new lesson
   - Discussing new concepts and practicing new skills #1 (Explicit Teaching / Modeling)
   - Discussing new concepts and practicing new skills #2 (Guided Practice)
   - Developing mastery (Independent Practice / Formative Assessment)
3. **After the Lesson**:
   - Finding practical applications of concepts and skills in daily living (Valuing / Application)
   - Making generalizations and abstractions about the lesson (Generalization)
   - Evaluating learning (Formative Assessment)
   - Additional activities for application or remediation (Assignment / Agreement)

### V. Remarks & Reflection (Mga Tala at Pagninilay)
- Number of learners who earned 80% in evaluation
- Number of learners requiring remediation
- Teaching strategies that worked well and difficulties encountered

## 2. Multilingual Lesson Support

Lessons in GuroDesk support:
- **English**: Standard English terminology with clear DepEd instructional phrasing.
- **Filipino**: Pure Filipino terms (e.g. *Pamamaraan*, *Pagganyak*, *Paglalahat*, *Pagtataya*).
- **Mother Tongue / Local Language**: Regional languages (e.g. Tagalog, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon, Bikol, Waray) for Primary grades (K to 3).

## 3. Lesson Plan Export & Template Compatibility

- **Word (.docx)**: Supports standard DepEd table-based DLL/DLP layouts and teacher-uploaded custom `.docx` templates with semantic field mapping.
- **PDF**: Renders pagination with continuation headers and keep-with-next table rows.
- **Browser Print**: Optimized 10pt typography with CSS page-break protections.
